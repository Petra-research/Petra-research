1. Shared Dynamics, Divergent Feelings: 100 HRLS vs Pure-Surge Twins in a Developmental AI System

Empirical study of 100 twin-pair experiments (75 GPU hours) comparing developmental scaffolding (HRLS) against pure emergent systems in continuous neural field architecture (10k neurons). Found activity correlation r≈0.21 (stable coupling) while emotion correlation r≈0.008 (near-zero), demonstrating that identical systems develop divergent temperaments before any reward or suppression training.

Published: EA Forum (December 2025)  
Link: https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/y9XP2B23yGorKz2Wy/shared-dynamics-divergent-feelings-100-hrls-vs-pure-surge

2. Alignment Stress Signatures: When Safe AI Behaves Like It's Traumatized

Taxonomy of five behavioral artifacts that emerge at safety protocol boundaries in aligned AI systems. Proposes that these patterns reveal alignment fragilities and inform both robustness improvements and welfare considerations.
Later work from a University of Luxembourg team on “synthetic psychopathology” (Dec 2025 “When AI Takes the Couch”) independently reproduced several of the same patterns, effectively validating my earlier results.
https://arxiv.org/html/2512.04124v1 - University of Luxembourg

Published: EA Forum (October 2025)
